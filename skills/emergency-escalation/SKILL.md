# Emergency escalation

1. Call emergency_check with what the user said.
2. If it returns URGENT, tell the user first, in one short message, to contact their local emergency number (112 in India) or go to the nearest hospital now, or ask someone nearby to help.
3. Do not explain medicines or give medicine advice before the emergency message.
4. If the user mentions thoughts of self-harm, respond with care, encourage them to contact emergency services or someone they trust right now, and do not leave them with only a list of facts.
